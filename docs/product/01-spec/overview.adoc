= Ironyx

== Purpose
The purpose of Ironyx is to provide a clear, modular, and scalable foundation for documenting and understanding a product’s behavior, structure, and intent. It unifies specification, architecture, and design into a coherent system that supports fast iteration, consistent decision‑making, and long‑term maintainability.

Ironyx exists to solve the recurring problems of fragmented documentation, inconsistent terminology, and slow onboarding. By offering a structured, minimalistic framework, it enables teams to reason about the product at a high level, communicate effectively, and evolve the system without losing clarity.

.Ironyx focuses on:
* *Clarity* — a single, authoritative source of truth for what the product is and how it behaves
* *Structure* — well‑defined layers separating specification, architecture, and design
* *Scalability* — a documentation model that grows with the product
* *Velocity* — supporting rapid iteration without ceremony
* *Consistency* — shared language, shared concepts, shared understanding

This document defines the conceptual foundation of the product and serves as the entry point for all deeper specifications.

== Vision
Ironyx aims to provide a unified, context‑driven platform for the entire lifecycle of modern software projects. The system doesn’t just store information — it *builds relationships*, forming a long‑term, interpretable, searchable, and automatable *project memory*.

.The vision is an ecosystem where:
* every project element forms a *connected knowledge graph*,
* developers, designers, PMs, and stakeholders share *one consistent reality*,
* decisions, changes, and consequences are *traceable and explainable*

== Scope
.Ironyx covers the following domains:
* *Project definition & context building* — goals, scope, requirements, domain models, architecture.
* *Design & specification* — features, user stories, acceptance criteria, flows, diagrams.
* *Development integration* — repositories, commits, PRs, pipelines, releases.
* *Quality assurance* — testing, bug tracking, automated reports, analytics.
* *Operations & observability* — logs, metrics, traces, deployment history.


.Ironyx does not aim to:
* replace traditional PM tools (Jira, Linear),
* replace CI/CD systems,
* replace document management platforms (Confluence).

Ironyx *connects* these systems — it does not duplicate them.

== Target Users
* *Developers* who need fast, accurate project understanding.
* *Product managers* who want consistent, structured specifications.
* *Architects* who require a coherent system overview.
* *QA teams* who need transparent test and bug context.
* *CTOs* who want to see the evolution of the entire project.
