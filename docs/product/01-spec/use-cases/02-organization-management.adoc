= Organization Management (ORG)

== UC-ORG-001 — Create Organization

An authenticated user creates a new organization in *Ironyx*. The system collects the required information, such as the organization name and optional descriptive details, and validates the input. Once the data is confirmed, *Ironyx* creates the organization as an isolated workspace with its own configuration space and data boundaries.

The user who creates the organization is automatically receiving full administrative privileges. After the organization is created, the user is redirected to the organization dashboard, where they can inviting team members, and configuring the organization’s structure.

== UC-ORG-002 — Invite User to Organization

An organization administrator invites a new user to join the organization. The administrator provides the email address of the invitee and assigns them a role that defines their permissions within the organization. *Ironyx* sends an email invitation to the specified address, containing a link to accept the invitation.

When the invitee clicks the link, they are prompted to sign in through the external identity provider. Upon successful authentication, *Ironyx* verifies the user's identity, checks the invitation status, and grants them access to the organization according to the permissions defined in the invitation.

== UC-ORG-003 — Manage Organization Settings

An _Organization _Administrator_ accesses the organization settings to update configuration options. These settings include modifying the organization name, updating the description, and managing the list of users associated with the organization. *Ironyx* validates all submitted changes and saves them as part of the organization’s configuration.

When managing users, the _Administrator_ can adjust role assignments within the organization. *Administrator*, *Product Owner*, *Business Analyst*, *UX Designer*, *Developer*, *QA Engineer* roles can be granted by an Organization _Administrator_. Any changes to user roles or organization details are applied immediately, and *Ironyx* may notify affected users when necessary.

This Use Case ensures that organization-level configuration remains controlled, auditable, and aligned with the organization’s administrative policies.

== UC-ORG-004 — Define Organization Teams

An Organization _Administrator_ defines teams within the organization.

Teams such as *Developer Team* and *QA Engineer Team* can be created at the organization level.

After defining a team, the _Administrator_ can assign users to the team and manage team membership.

*Ironyx* validates all team configurations and ensures that users have appropriate access based on their team assignments.

== UC-ORG-005 — Deactivate User in Organization

An _Organization _Administrator_ deactivates a user’s access to the organization. The administrator selects the user from the list of organization members and initiates the deactivation process. *Ironyx* prompts the administrator to confirm the action, emphasizing that deactivation will prevent the user from accessing any products or data within the organization. Upon confirmation, *Ironyx* updates the user’s status to deactivated, effectively revoking their access to the organization. The deactivated user receives a notification about the change in their access status. 

_Product Owner_ only can be deactivated if they does not have any active products. If the _Product Owner_ has active products, the system will require the administrator to transfer ownership of those products to another _Product Owner_ before allowing deactivation. This ensures that all products remain managed and accessible within the organization, even if the original owner is deactivated.

== UC-ORG-006 — Reactivate User in Organization

An _Organization _Administrator_ reactivates a previously deactivated user’s access to the organization. The administrator selects the user from the list of deactivated members and initiates the reactivation process. *Ironyx* prompts the administrator to confirm the action, ensuring that they understand the implications of reactivating the user’s access. Upon confirmation, *Ironyx* updates the user’s status to active, restoring their access to all products and data within the organization. The reactivated user receives a notification about their restored access.

== UC-ORG-007 — Delete User from Organization

An _Organization _Administrator_ deletes a user from the organization. The administrator selects the user from the list of organization members and initiates the deletion process. *Ironyx* prompts the administrator to confirm the action, emphasizing that deletion is irreversible and will permanently remove the user’s access to all products and data within the organization. Upon confirmation, *Ironyx* deletes the user’s account from the organization, revoking all access and permissions. The deleted user receives a notification about their removal from the organization.

_Product Owner_ only can be deleted if they does not have any active products. If the _Product Owner_ has active products, the system will require the administrator to transfer ownership of those products to another _Product Owner_ before allowing deletion. This ensures that all products remain managed and accessible within the organization, even if the original owner is deleted.

== UC-ORG-008 — Transfer Product Ownership

An _Organization _Administrator_ initiates the transfer of product ownership from one _Product Owner_ to another. The administrator selects the product and the current owner, then chooses a new owner from the list of active _Product Owners_ within the organization. *Ironyx* validates that the new owner has the necessary permissions to manage the product and prompts the administrator to confirm the transfer. Upon confirmation, *Ironyx* updates the product’s ownership information, granting the new owner full control over the product while revoking ownership privileges from the previous owner. Both the previous and new owners receive notifications about the change in ownership status.

== UC-ORG-009 — Delete Organization

An _Organization _Administrator_ initiates the deletion of an organization. The system prompts the administrator to confirm the action, emphasizing that this operation is irreversible and will permanently remove all data, products, and configurations associated with the organization. Upon confirmation, *Ironyx* deletes the organization and all its related data from the system. The administrator is then redirected to the landing page, and any users who were part of the organization receive notifications about the deletion. This Use Case ensures that organization removal is handled securely and with clear communication to all affected parties.
