= Domain Area Model

== Purpose

A *Domain Area* represents a distinct business or functional segment within a Product.  
It defines the boundaries, responsibilities, and conceptual scope of a specific part of the system.

Domain Areas help structure the Product into clear, manageable units that reflect real business logic rather than technical modules.

- Provide a clear conceptual separation of business responsibilities.
- Define the scope and boundaries of each functional area.
- Organize Features and Documents under meaningful business contexts.
- Support ownership and contributor assignment at a domain level.
- Improve clarity, maintainability, and onboarding for complex Products.

== Core Entities

The Product Model consists of the following primary entities:
- Feature

== Attributes

- `name`: The name of the domain area (e.g., "User Management", "Billing", "Authentication").
- `description`: A concise explanation of what the domain area covers.
- `responsibility`: The business responsibilities and purpose of this domain area.
- `started_at`: The date when this domain area became part of the Product's lifecycle.

== Relationships

- 1 Product → N Domain Areas  
- 1 Domain Area → N Features
