= Overview
:toc: macro
:toclevels: 1

Software teams work across fragmented tools, scattered documents, and inconsistent communication channels.
Critical decisions, specifications, and workflows become disconnected, making it difficult to maintain clarity, alignment, and momentum throughout the development lifecycle.

*Ironyx* is built for _Product Owners_, _Business Analysts_, _UX Designers_, _Developers_, _Testers_, and _Stakeholders_ who need a unified, context‑driven environment to collaborate and understand the evolving state of a project.
It provides a single, structured platform where every decision, requirement, workflow, and artifact is connected, traceable, and always presented in the right context for each role.

Teams gain a shared understanding of their product, reduce ambiguity, accelerate decision‑making, and build software with greater confidence, consistency, and clarity.
Ironyx transforms project chaos into a coherent, navigable system that supports the entire xref:99-glossary.adoc#sdlc[SDLC] from vision to release.
