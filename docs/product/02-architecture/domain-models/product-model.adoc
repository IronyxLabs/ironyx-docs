= Product Model

== Purpose

The Product Model defines the conceptual structure of a product within the Ironyx SDLC framework.  
It describes the core entities, their attributes, and the relationships that form the backbone of product planning, execution, and documentation.

This document is formal, structural, and non-narrative.

== Core Entities

The Product Model consists of the following primary entities:

- Product
- <<domain-area-model.adoc,Domain Area>>
- Roles
- Initiative
- Roadmap Item
- Decision
- Document
- Version
- Owner
- Contributors

Each entity is defined in detail below.

== Entity Definitions

=== Product

Represents the top-level container for all product-related artifacts.

==== Attributes
- `name`: Product name.
- `logo`: Logo of the product. Optional.
- `description`: High-level description.
- `status`: Vision, Active, Support, Not Supported, Retired.
- `started_at`: When the product started as vision.

==== Relationships
- 1 Product → N Domain Areas
- 1 Product → N Initiatives
- 1 Product → N Roadmap Items
- 1 Product → N Decisions
- 1 Product → N Documents
- 1 Product → 1 Owner
- 1 Product → N Contributors

---

=== Domain Area

A logical, bounded context within the product.  
Each Domain Area encapsulates its own rules, entities, and responsibilities.

==== Attributes
- `id`
- `name`
- `description`
- `scope`
- `owner`
- `created_at`
- `updated_at`

==== Relationships
- 1 Domain Area → N Features
- 1 Domain Area → N Documents
- Domain Area → Domain Area (dependencies)

---

=== Feature

A concrete capability of the product.  
Each Feature belongs to exactly one Domain Area.

==== Attributes
- `id`
- `name`
- `description`
- `acceptance_criteria`
- `workflow_description`
- `status` (planned, in-progress, completed)
- `created_at`
- `updated_at`
